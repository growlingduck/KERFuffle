# Quick start: your first job

For the people using KERFuffle in the shop. Six steps from an empty app to a
printed cut list.

The app has the same steps under **Help** (the button at the top right, or
**Settings → Help**), with a link to each place and a list of the words and
symbols KERFuffle uses. **Print quick start** there prints both on one page to
pin up by the saw.

## 1. Check the shop defaults

Open **Settings → Construction** and look over the panel thickness, toe kick,
countertop and how your cabinets are built. Every new job copies these. A job
you've already made keeps its own copy, so changing a default later never
changes an old cut list. Click **Save defaults** if you changed anything, or
**They're right** on the Jobs screen if you didn't.

## 2. Start a new job

On the **Jobs** screen, click **New job** (or press **Ctrl+N**). Give it a
name and the customer, check how it's built, and click **Create and open**.

## 3. Enter the sizes

The job opens in the **Workspace**. Type each cabinet's width, height and
depth in inches. All of these work: `23 3/4`, `23-3/4` or `23.75`, with or
without the `"`. The arrow keys ↑ and ↓ nudge a size by 1/16″, and with Shift
by 1″.

## 4. Finished ends, shelves and quantity

For each cabinet, pick the ends that show (finished ends get laminate), the
number of shelves, and how many of that cabinet to build. **Add cabinet** (or
**Ctrl+N** in the Workspace) adds the next one.

## 5. Check the cut list, layouts and estimate

Click **Cut list →** (or the **Output** tab). The **Cut list** lists every part. **Layouts** shows the
parts placed on sheets, and **Estimate** shows what to buy and what it costs.

## 6. Print or save as PDF

On **Output**, **Print…** (or **Ctrl+P**) prints what's showing: the cut
list, the layouts or the estimate. **Save PDF…** saves the same pages as a file.

## Want to look around first?

**Settings → Help → Add the example job** adds a small kitchen built with your
shop defaults. It's an ordinary job: change it, print it, archive it.
